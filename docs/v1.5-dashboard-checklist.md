## Gotchas that ate time during setup

0. **Paid Apps Agreement (the actual rejection)**. v1.5 first submission was approved into review then rejected under Guideline 2.1(b) "Information Needed" because Apple's sandbox session returned ZERO products to the app — `Purchases.getOfferings()` resolved to empty. Cause: the Account Holder had never signed the Paid Apps Agreement (we previously only sold via Stripe for Mac, which doesn't require it). Without that agreement, Apple's IAP infrastructure literally won't surface any products to anyone, in or out of sandbox. Fix: navigate to `appstoreconnect.apple.com/business`, sign Paid Apps Agreement, fill MRDP compliance info (answer "No" to "Do any of your apps provide personal services?" for digital goods apps), then reply to App Review via the Messages thread on the submission detail page + click "Update Review" + "Resubmit to App Review". Status flips Rejected → Ready for Review → Waiting for Review.
   **Pre-flight for next first-IAP launch**: visit Business page BEFORE submitting. Confirm Paid Apps Agreement is Active (no "Sign the Paid Apps Agreement" link visible), Bank Account is Active, Tax Forms are Active, MRDP compliance is filled, Digital Services Act compliance is filled. No yellow "Missing Info" badges on any row.

1. **Group localization** (see Section 2 above) — the single biggest setup unblocker (different from the Paid Apps issue). Fix immediately when you see "Missing Metadata" on Sub products that look complete.
2. **`.v2` product ID suffix** — Apple reserves deleted product IDs forever. Never delete IAPs unless you're willing to burn the ID.
3. **Stripe Portal config isolation** for multi-product accounts (LinkJolt + Dash share `acct_1MXohHKXxPB1KCuR`).
4. **`ENTITLEMENT_REQUIRED=false` during review** — Apple reviewer's sandbox session needs sync to work without an active sub or they'll mark the IAP as non-functional. Flip true ONLY after approval.
5. **France** — excluded from IAP availability. Encryption export docs not filed.
6. **`cap sync ios` pod install fails locally** — `ffi` gem version mismatch with bundler. Workaround: skip the script, manually `next build` + `cp -R out/. ios/App/App/public/` + xcodebuild directly. Pods/ stays committed and in sync.
7. **First IAP submission bundles with version implicitly** — the "In-App Purchases and Subscriptions" section on the version page only appears once IAPs are "Ready to Submit". If you submit version-only before fixing IAP metadata, it goes through silently with no IAPs — easy to miss.
8. **Mac platform note for the tag**: `.github/workflows/build-and-release.yml` is macOS-only (`runs-on: macos-latest`, `npm run release:mac`). Pushing the tag does NOT build Win/Linux despite the codebase supporting them. Memory.md called out Mac-only.
